zines

Ponyo

Zine based on the film Ponyo. The design is focused on strong imagery and colors that resemeble the film. The layout is defined by the film's whimsical yet calming nature, combining some areas of tension that still works with the overall layout.


Arcane

Zine based on the animated series Arcane. The design incorporates the show's distinctive color schemes and rough aesthetic. There is a an emphasis on strong typography to match with the visual power of the stills taken from the show. The design is rather scrapbook-like to match the artistic and experimental animation style.


Dune

Zine based on the film Dune. The design is emphasized by its bold use of type which are based on lines taken from the film. Typography is heavily focused because of the significance of the quotes, mixed with the neutral sand colored stills.
