Witch's Brew

A hot sauce packaging design with a focus on bold colors that reflect the spicy nature of hot sauce. The brand is based on the asethetics of a witch and her brew. The typography is a mixture of blackletter and serifs to match the fierce but magical essence of the hot sauce.


Midnight Reserve

A liquor label and bottle design that is based on the imagery of the moon. The lid of the bottle is designed after the moon. The design incorporates elegance with its use of illustrative vine and grape elements. The color palette is based on the hues of the night sky and the moon.
