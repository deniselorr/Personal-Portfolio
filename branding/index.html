branding

Past Life Archive

Promotional event branding hosted by Past Life Archive, a vintage store that curates unique pieces for its customers. The branding for the event was designed with the focus of an illustrative style, to match the creative ideology behind collecting antiques. The colors were inspired by vintage fruit stickers.


Espurra Coffee & Pastry

Espurra is a warm and inviting space where coffee and comfort come together. The brand's goal is to create a cafe experience that feels both familiar and special, with a focus on community, warmth, and playfullness. Espurra's mission is to offer not just great coffee, but a space that feels like home. The logo is a cat because they are known to be cozy, calming animals, and they encapsulate the brand's essence.


Thee Sacred Souls Album Cover

Album cover design for Thee Sacred Souls album, with a focused imagery of strong feelings of love, which is what the album explores. There is a strong emphasis of vintage aesthetics to match the band's soulful aesthetic. The typography is a mixture of script and a custom font to match the tension of the imagery while representing the feeling of love; chaotic and beautiful.
